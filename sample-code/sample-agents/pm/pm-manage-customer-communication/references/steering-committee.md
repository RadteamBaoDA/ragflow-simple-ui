# Steering committee

## Use when

Use this variant when executive governance must decide or unblock material matters.

## Task guidance

For **Manage Customer Communication**, emphasize business impact, options, recommendation, decision owner, deadline, and consequences of delay.

## Decision checks

- Confirm owners, dates, dependencies, confidence, and approval state.
- Do not treat coordination evidence as a specialist delivery gate.
- If multiple references apply, reconcile their constraints explicitly instead of merging incompatible advice.
