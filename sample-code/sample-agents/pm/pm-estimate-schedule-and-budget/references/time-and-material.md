# Time-and-material delivery

## Use when

Use this variant when capacity and actual consumption drive cost.

## Task guidance

For **Estimate Schedule and Budget**, emphasize rate assumptions, capacity range, burn forecast, reprioritization cadence, and budget alerts.

## Decision checks

- Confirm owners, dates, dependencies, confidence, and approval state.
- Do not treat coordination evidence as a specialist delivery gate.
- If multiple references apply, reconcile their constraints explicitly instead of merging incompatible advice.
