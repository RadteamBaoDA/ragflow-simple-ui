# Dedicated-team delivery

## Use when

Use this variant when a stable customer-funded team owns a rolling backlog.

## Task guidance

For **Estimate Schedule and Budget**, emphasize team capacity, role mix, ramp-up, utilization, backlog readiness, and governance cadence.

## Decision checks

- Confirm owners, dates, dependencies, confidence, and approval state.
- Do not treat coordination evidence as a specialist delivery gate.
- If multiple references apply, reconcile their constraints explicitly instead of merging incompatible advice.
