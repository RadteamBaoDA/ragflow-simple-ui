# Emergency change

## Use when

Use this variant when delay creates immediate operational or business harm.

## Task guidance

For **Manage Scope and Change Requests**, emphasize minimum safe scope, delegated authority, risk acceptance, rollback, retrospective documentation, and later baseline repair.

## Decision checks

- Confirm owners, dates, dependencies, confidence, and approval state.
- Do not treat coordination evidence as a specialist delivery gate.
- If multiple references apply, reconcile their constraints explicitly instead of merging incompatible advice.
