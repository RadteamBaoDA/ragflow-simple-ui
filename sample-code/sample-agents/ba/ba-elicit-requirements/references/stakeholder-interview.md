# Stakeholder interview

## Use when

Use this variant when one or a few subject-matter experts hold needed knowledge.

## Task guidance

For **Elicit Requirements**, emphasize prepare role-specific questions, separate facts from opinions, confirm notes, and record unresolved conflicts.

## Decision checks

- Confirm the source, stakeholder, business rule, and unresolved conflict for each conclusion.
- Keep current state, desired state, proposal, and approved baseline distinct.
- If multiple references apply, reconcile their constraints explicitly instead of merging incompatible advice.
