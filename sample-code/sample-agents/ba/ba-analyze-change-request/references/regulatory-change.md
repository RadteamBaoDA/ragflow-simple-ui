# Regulatory change

## Use when

Use this variant when law, regulation, policy, or audit obligation drives the request.

## Task guidance

For **Analyze Change Request**, emphasize record authority and effective date, affected controls, evidence obligations, deadlines, and compliance owner.

## Decision checks

- Confirm the source, stakeholder, business rule, and unresolved conflict for each conclusion.
- Keep current state, desired state, proposal, and approved baseline distinct.
- If multiple references apply, reconcile their constraints explicitly instead of merging incompatible advice.
