# Business requirements document

## Use when

Use this variant when business sponsors need outcome, scope, and capability alignment.

## Task guidance

For **Write Requirement Specification**, emphasize emphasize objectives, stakeholders, scope, business capabilities, measures, assumptions, and approvals.

## Decision checks

- Confirm the source, stakeholder, business rule, and unresolved conflict for each conclusion.
- Keep current state, desired state, proposal, and approved baseline distinct.
- If multiple references apply, reconcile their constraints explicitly instead of merging incompatible advice.
