# Maintain Requirement Traceability

## Purpose

Produce a current requirement traceability matrix.

## Task responsibility

Own business analysis, requirement quality, and traceability. Do not make unapproved scope, architecture, implementation, test-pass, or commercial decisions.

## Core method

1. Specify **Requirement Inventory** as measurable, traceable, unambiguous statements with accountable ownership.
2. Resolve **Upstream Source** using task evidence, applicable constraints, alternatives, and explicit acceptance criteria.
3. Resolve **Design Mapping** using task evidence, applicable constraints, alternatives, and explicit acceptance criteria.
4. Trace and verify **Implementation Mapping** through entrypoints, dependencies, side effects, contracts, and focused tests.
5. Establish **Test Mapping** with reproducible cases, observed results, and uncovered paths.
6. Identify and rank **Coverage Gaps** by evidence, impact, likelihood, and affected owner.
7. Reconcile contradictions and state unresolved evidence, decision, and ownership gaps.

## Quality checks

- Tie each conclusion to a named stakeholder, source artifact, business rule, or labeled assumption.
- Keep business need, proposed scope, approved baseline, and technical design distinct.
- Confirm proposed changes are separated from approved baseline content.
- Confirm acceptance, approval, test, release, and customer decisions are claimed only with evidence.
