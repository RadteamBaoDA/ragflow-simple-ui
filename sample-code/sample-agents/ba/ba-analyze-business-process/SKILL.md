# Analyze Business Process

## Purpose

Produce a business process analysis with gaps and measurable outcomes.

## Task responsibility

Own business analysis, requirement quality, and traceability. Do not make unapproved scope, architecture, implementation, test-pass, or commercial decisions.

## Reference routing

Classify the subtype before detailed work. Read only the matching reference; combine references only when the request spans multiple observable subtypes.

- Read [As-is process](references/as-is-process.md) when the current operating model must be understood.
- Read [To-be process](references/to-be-process.md) when a target operating model is being designed.
- Read [Gap analysis](references/gap-analysis.md) when current and target processes must be compared.

## Core method

1. Define **Actors and Boundaries** precisely, including exclusions, owners, interfaces, and assumptions.
2. Resolve **Process Steps** using task evidence, applicable constraints, alternatives, and explicit acceptance criteria.
3. Specify **Business Rules** as measurable, traceable, unambiguous statements with accountable ownership.
4. Identify and rank **Pain Points** by evidence, impact, likelihood, and affected owner.
5. Identify and rank **Gap Analysis** by evidence, impact, likelihood, and affected owner.
6. Specify **Target Outcomes** as measurable, traceable, unambiguous statements with accountable ownership.
7. Reconcile contradictions and state unresolved evidence, decision, and ownership gaps.

## Quality checks

- Tie each conclusion to a named stakeholder, source artifact, business rule, or labeled assumption.
- Keep business need, proposed scope, approved baseline, and technical design distinct.
- Confirm proposed changes are separated from approved baseline content.
- Confirm acceptance, approval, test, release, and customer decisions are claimed only with evidence.
